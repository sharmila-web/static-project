Masala Dosa with Chutneys

Crispy South Indian rice-and-lentil crepes filled with a spiced, aromatic potato mash. Served alongside refreshing, low-fat homemade chutneys for a satisfying breakfast or light dinner.

Meal type: Breakfast

Total time: 40 minutes

Serves: 2

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Heat 2 teaspoons of oil in a pan. Add the mustard seeds and curry leaves until they splutter.
  2. the sliced onions and sauté until translucent.
  3. Stir in the turmeric powder, then add the mashed potatoes and a splash of water. Mix well and cook for 3 minutes, then set the potato filling aside.
  4. Heat a flat non-stick griddle (tawa) over medium heat. Splash a little water on it to cool it down, then wipe it clean.
  5. Pour a ladleful of fermented dosa batter into the center of the pan and spread it outward in a circular motion to form a thin crepe. Drizzle a tiny drop of oil around the edges.
  6. Cook until the bottom turns golden brown and crispy. Place a scoop of the potato filling in the center, fold the crepe over, and serve hot with the fresh coconut chutney.

Cooking Tips:

  • To achieve a perfectly thin and crispy wrap every time, make sure the griddle is wiped down with a damp cloth between making each pancake to control its temperature.
