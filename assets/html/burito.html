Loaded Burrito

A hearty, high-fiber flour tortilla packed tight with protein-packed chicken, seasoned rice, fiber-rich black beans, and melted cheese toasted to absolute crispy perfection.

Meal type: Main course

Total time: 50 minutes

Serves: 4

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Saute Veggies & Meat: Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Sauté the diced onions and bell peppers for 3-4 minutes until softened.
  2. Season the Mix: Add the shredded chicken, black beans, corn, and taco seasoning to the skillet. Toss well and cook for another 3 minutes until heated through.
  3. Assemble Burrito: Lay each tortilla flat on a clean surface. Place a 1/4 cup layer of cooked rice in the center, followed by a generous portion of the skillet chicken mixture and a sprinkle of cheese.
  4. Roll and Toast: Fold the left and right sides of the tortilla over the filling, then roll tightly from the bottom upward to seal it. Place the burritos seam-side down in a hot, dry skillet over medium-high heat for 1-2 minutes per side until gold and crispy.

Cooking Tips:

  • Press a shallow indentation into the center of each raw chicken patty before baking; this prevents the burger from puffing up in the middle and ensures even cooking.
