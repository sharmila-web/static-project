Hakka Noodles

An Indo-Chinese classic packed with colorful julienned vegetables and tossed on high heat. This recipe focuses on minimizing added sodium and processed oils while maximizing vegetable volume.

Meal type:starter

Total time: 30 minutes

Serves: 2 to 3

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Boil the noodles according to the package instructions, keeping them slightly firm (al dente). Drain, rinse with cold water, and toss with a drop of oil to prevent sticking.
  2. Heat 2 teaspoons of oil in a large wok or wide frying pan over high heat. Add the minced garlic and spring onion whites, stir-frying for 30 seconds.
  3. Toss in the cabbage, carrots, and bell peppers. Stir-fry rapidly on high heat for 2–3 minutes so the vegetables retain their crisp texture.
  4. Add the drained noodles to the pan. Drizzle the soy sauce and white vinegar evenly over the top, then season with cracked black pepper.
  5. Toss everything together vigorously for 1–2 minutes until heated through. Garnish with spring onion greens and serve hot.

Cooking Tips:

  • Keep your pan or wok as hot as possible throughout the cooking process. Cooking the vegetables rapidly over high heat gives them a classic smoky street-style edge without turning them soggy.
