Chopped Salad

A bright, high-fiber side dish emphasizing crisp raw vegetables and a simple zesty dressing. This salad adds crunch and volume to meals without relying on heavy cream-based toppings.

Meal type: snack

Total time: 10 minutes

Serves: 4

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Chop the cucumber, cherry tomatoes, bell pepper, and onion into small, evenly sized pieces.
  2. Combine all the chopped vegetables together in a large serving bowl.
  3. In a small cup, whisk together the fresh lemon juice, extra-virgin olive oil, dried oregano, and pepper.
  4. Pour the dressing over the salad base and toss thoroughly to ensure all the vegetable pieces are coated evenly. Serve cold.

Cooking Tips:

  • Chop your vegetables into very small, uniform cubes (about the size of a chickpea). Ensuring the pieces are uniform helps distribute the simple dressing evenly, meaning every forkful delivers a balanced blend of flavors.
