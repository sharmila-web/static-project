Mango Milkshake

A refreshing and creamy beverage that relies entirely on the natural sweetness of ripe seasonal mangoes. Made using skimmed or plant-based milks to keep the recipe refreshing and heart-healthy.

Meal type: Drink

Total time: 30 minutes

Serves: 1 to 2

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Chop the fresh mango flesh into small, uniform cubes.
  2. Transfer the mango pieces into a high-speed blender.
  3. Pour in the chilled milk of your choice and add the ground cardamom.
  4. Blend on high speed for 1–2 minutes until the texture is smooth, frothy, and completely free of lumps.
  5. Pour into tall glasses, garnish with a scattering of chopped pistachios or a fresh mint leaf, and serve cold.

Cooking Tips:

  • For a thicker, ice-cream-like consistency without adding fat or sugar, freeze your chopped mango cubes for an hour before blending them with the milk.
