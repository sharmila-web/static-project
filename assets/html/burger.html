Crisp Chicken-Burger

A lightened-up twist on a fast-food staple, utilizing lean minced meat and oven-baking methods to keep fat content down. The result yields a crispy exterior without the need for deep frying.

Meal type: fast food

Total time: 40 minutes

Serves: 4

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a baking tray with greaseproof paper.
  2. In a bowl, thoroughly mix the minced chicken, garlic powder, onion powder, and half of the beaten egg. Shape the mixture into 4 uniform patties.
  3. Mix the breadcrumbs and grated parmesan in a shallow dish. Dip each patty into the remaining egg, then coat evenly with the breadcrumb mixture.
  4. Arrange on the baking tray and bake for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden brown and cooked through.
  5. Toast the wholemeal buns lightly, place a chicken patty on each, and garnish with lettuce and tomato slices.

Cooking Tips:

  • Press a shallow indentation into the center of each raw chicken patty before baking; this prevents the burger from puffing up in the middle and ensures even cooking.
