Chocolate Strawberry Fudge Cake

A decadent dessert choice that cuts down on heavy butter and refined sugars by leaning on fruit purées and high-quality dark cocoa. Rich fudge textures pair nicely with sweet, fresh strawberries.

Meal type:Dessert

Total time: 1 Hour

Serves: 4 to 8

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a round cake tin with greaseproof baking paper.
  2. Whisk together the pastry flour, dark cocoa powder, and baking powder in a large mixing bowl.
  3. In a separate bowl, blend the maple syrup, applesauce, and eggs together. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients, folding gently until just combined.
  4. Pour the batter into the cake tin and bake for 25–30 minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean. Allow it to cool.
  5. Once cooled, layer the fresh strawberry slices across the top of the cake and finish with a decorative drizzle of the melted dark chocolate chips.

Cooking Tips:

  • Be careful not to over-mix the cake batter once you combine the wet and dry ingredients. Overworking wholemeal flour activates the gluten too much, which can result in a dense, heavy sponge rather than a soft, fudgy texture.
