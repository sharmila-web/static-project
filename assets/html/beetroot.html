Beetroot Hummus

A vibrant, earth-toned dip that updates traditional chickpea spread with sweet, cooked beets. Perfect as a quick starter, party dip, or spread on flatbreads.

Meal type: side dish

Total time: 15 minutes

Serves: 2

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Place the drained chickpeas, chopped beetroot, garlic, olive oil, ground cumin, and ground coriander into a food processor.
  2. Add the lemon juice and a generous pinch of black pepper.
  3. Blend all the ingredients into a thick, slightly coarse paste.
  4. Transfer to a bowl and serve immediately with raw vegetable sticks or toasted wholemeal pitta bread.

Cooking Tips:

  • If the mixture feels a bit too dry or thick while blending, add a splash of water or a few extra drops of lemon juice instead of pouring in more oil.
