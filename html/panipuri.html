Golgappa(Pani Puri)

A refreshing and crisp Indian street food classic featuring hollowed crispy puris filled with a spiced chickpea-potato mixture and completely submerged in tangy mint-coriander water.

Meal type:snack , starter

Total time: 50 minutes

Serves: 4

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Prepare the Filling: In a mixing bowl, combine the mashed potatoes and boiled chickpeas. Mix in half of the chaat masala, cumin powder, and regular salt. Set aside.
  2. Make the Teekha Pani (Spicy Water): Blend the mint, coriander, green chilies, and ginger into a smooth paste using a splash of water.
  3. Assemble the Water: Pour the green paste into a large pitcher with the 4 cups of cold filtered water. Stir in the tamarind paste, black salt, remaining chaat masala, and roasted cumin powder. Adjust seasonings to taste.
  4. Arrange on the baking tray and bake for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden brown and cooked through.
  5. Serve: Gently poke a hole in the center of each crispy puri shell. Stuff with a small spoonful of the filling, submerge or pour the spiced water inside, and consume immediately.

Cooking Tips:

  • Press a shallow indentation into the center of each raw chicken patty before baking; this prevents the burger from puffing up in the middle and ensures even cooking.
