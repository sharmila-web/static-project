Steamed Dumplings (Momos)

Delicate, pleated parcels filled with a finely minced, seasoned vegetable mixture. Steaming these pockets keeps them light and eliminates the heavy fats associated with pan-fried alternatives.

Meal type: snack,starter

Total time: 45 minutes

Serves: 2 to 4

🍳Cooking Steps:

  1. Heat a tiny drop of oil in a pan and quickly sauté the minced ginger, garlic, cabbage, carrots, and mushrooms for 2 minutes until slightly softened. Stir in the soy sauce and let it cool.
  2. Divide the rested dough into small, equal balls. Roll each ball out on a lightly floured surface into a thin, translucent circle, keeping the edges slightly thinner than the center.
  3. Place a spoonful of the cooled vegetable mixture into the center of a dough wrapper.
  4. Pleat and gather the edges together tightly at the top to seal the filling completely inside.
  5. Arrange the dumplings in a lightly greased steamer basket. Steam over boiling water for 10–12 minutes until the wrappers look shiny and no longer feel sticky to the touch.

Cooking Tips:

  • When rolling out the dumpling wrappers, try to keep the edges slightly thinner than the center. This design feature ensures that when you gather and pleat the dough at the top, the joint doesn't end up too thick or doughy, helping the dumpling cook through evenly.
